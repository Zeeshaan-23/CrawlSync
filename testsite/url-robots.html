Politeness & The Robots Exclusion Protocol

Respecting site owner crawling policies with robots.txt.

Ethical Crawling Standards

The Robots Exclusion Protocol (specified via /robots.txt) instructs web crawlers which paths are permissible for automated retrieval:

User-agent: CrawlSync
Disallow: /admin/
Disallow: /private/
Crawl-delay: 1

In addition to robots.txt, polite crawlers enforce request rate limits and concurrency caps to prevent accidental denial-of-service on target web servers.
